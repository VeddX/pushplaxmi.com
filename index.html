About Our Factory

DIVYAKIRTI and DIVYASHREE are consumer and commercial brands manufactured under Pushplaxmi Technopure Pvt Limited. We operate an automated, fully mechanical milling unit delivering nutritious flours to retail homes and wholesale markets.

By blending traditional stone-ground milling principles with industrial cleaning machinery, every batch retains its natural grain fibers, core nutrients, and authentic purity.

100% Pure

Completely free from added preservatives or blending fillers.
